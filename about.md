# About Joost

## Creating experiences beyond the screen

I am a concept creator and developer for Virtual Reality applications in sports and human motion. I develop VR games and interactive experiences, combining creative concepts with practical development.

I am interested in how immersive technologies can create new ways to experience sports, practise skills, understand movement and interact with digital environments.

### Areas of interest

- Virtual Reality and Augmented Reality
- Sports and performance
- Movement analysis
- Interactive training
- Rehabilitation
- Human interaction

### Technology

**Unity** is the game engine used across the projects featured in this portfolio.

This portfolio focuses on the experiences and results. Project-specific details, visuals and videos will be added as they are prepared for publication.
