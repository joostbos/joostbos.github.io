# Friend or Enemy

**360° Video · Safety Awareness · Life Coaching**

Friend or Enemy is an educational application designed to support girls who have been exposed to sexual violence. Through immersive 360-degree video scenarios, the experience explores situations that can occur in everyday life and offers clues about recognising risk and considering ways to get to safety.

The aim is to create a space to recognise potentially unsafe situations, think through possible responses and understand that it is possible to seek help and report abuse.

## Explore situations in 360°

The application uses 360-degree videos to place the viewer inside scenarios based on frequently occurring situations.

The scenarios provide cues and ideas about how someone might respond or leave a situation that feels unsafe. The immersive format is intended to make the situations more recognisable and encourage reflection on possible choices.

## Understanding sextortion and reporting

One of the scenarios focuses on sextortion. It shows a situation in which the victim reports what is happening and the story depicts the perpetrator being arrested by the police.

This scenario is intended to reinforce that reporting sextortion is an option and that seeking help can lead to action. The depicted arrest is part of the scenario; real-world outcomes depend on the circumstances and the authorities involved.
