# Playground

**Children · Physical Education · Motor Skills · Movement**

Playground is an interactive application designed to encourage children to move more. Developed for use in primary schools during Physical Education (PE) classes, it turns practising fundamental movement skills into an engaging activity.

The experience is built around recommendations for children's motor learning. It gives children opportunities to practise a range of movement skills through active play.

## Movement skills

Playground covers important fundamental motor skills, including:

- **Jumping** — practising different ways of taking off and landing.
- **Dodging** — moving quickly and changing direction to avoid obstacles or other moving elements.
- **Catching** — developing timing and hand-eye coordination.
- **Throwing** — practising control, direction and coordination.

These activities help bring variety to PE lessons while encouraging children to develop a broad range of motor skills.

## Developed with MiTale

Playground was developed in collaboration with **MiTale**, a Finnish company, as part of a European project focused on innovation in health.

The collaboration brought together expertise to explore how interactive technology can contribute to children's movement and physical education.
