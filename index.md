# Joost Bosschert

## VR Concept Creator & Developer

**Virtual Reality · Sports · Human Motion · Interactive Experiences**

I am a concept creator and developer specialising in Virtual Reality applications for sports and human motion. I develop VR games and interactive experiences that bring digital environments and physical activity together.

My work explores how immersive technology can support sports training, movement analysis, rehabilitation and human interaction.
